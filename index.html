Minesweeper Guide

Mouse Controls:

  • Left-Click: Reveal a hidden square.
  • Right-Click: Place or remove a flag.
  • Double-Click / Chording: Click an opened number whose flags match its count to reveal remaining neighbors quickly!

Touch & Mobile:

  • Long Press (350ms): Quickly place or remove a flag.
  • Flag Mode Button: Toggle ON to reveal with flags using normal taps.

Rules:

  • Numbers indicate how many mines touch that cell.
  • The first click is always 100% safe and clears an opening area.
  • Clear all non-mine cells to win!
